What is Business OS?+

A standalone business-management system for operators who sell. Create and manage your team, define products and services, offer digital products, and give multiple people access — all in one cloud workspace.

₹1,20,000 one-time. Lifetime access to the system, with setup guidance included. No recurring software subscription for the product itself.

Who is it for?+

Business owners and operators who manage a sales team and a catalog of products or services — and want one system instead of scattered tools.

Does it include file storage or document hosting?+

No. Business OS focuses on team management, product and service setup, digital offerings, and multi-user access. It is not a file-storage or document-hosting platform.

How does multi-user access work?+

You create accounts for your team. Each person logs in with their own credentials. Data is cloud-based and stays synchronized so everyone works from the same system.
