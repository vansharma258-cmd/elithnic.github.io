What exactly is ELAS Business OS Lite?+

ELAS Lite is a one-time-acquisition Business Operating System for agencies and businesses. It includes CRM, lead management, client management, project operations, sales tracking, commission tracking, role-based access control, and Firebase-backed data — all in one unified platform.

What is the investment?+

ELAS Business OS Lite is available for a one-time payment of ₹1,00,000. This covers the complete OS with all modules and initial setup guidance.

Who is ELAS Lite for?+

Agencies, sales businesses, and operations teams that currently manage multiple tools (CRM, project tracker, spreadsheets for commission, etc.) and want a single unified system they own, not rent.

How does deployment work?+

We provide your admin credentials and deployment guidance. Your data lives in your own Firebase project. You have full ownership and control of your infrastructure.

How many users can I add?+

Unlimited users — add as many Admins, Managers, Closers, Delivery Partners, and Clients as your operation requires.

Is this a subscription?+

No. ELAS Lite is a one-time acquisition. After the initial payment, you own your OS. Firebase hosting costs (if any) are separate and minimal.

Can I customise modules?+

The Lite product ships with the eight standard modules as described. Customisation or additional modules can be negotiated as a separate engagement.
